About Us

Jarulbee India is a knowledge platform focused on product compliance, certification, testing and standards.

The platform aims to make regulatory and technical requirements easier to understand for manufacturers, importers, compliance professionals and other stakeholders.

What We Cover

Certifications

Information about BIS, WPC, BEE, EPR, TEC and MTCTE requirements.

Testing

Information about testing requirements, test procedures and laboratory considerations.

Standards

Practical information about applicable Indian and international standards.

Our Objective

Our objective is to simplify complex compliance information and provide a structured knowledge resource for understanding product requirements in the Indian market.
