TESTING

Testing that supports
the approval strategy.

Understand what to test, where to test, how samples should be handled and how technical evidence fits into the regulatory submission.

01

Test planning

Map product variants, applicable standards, test parameters and required evidence before sending samples.

02

Laboratory coordination

Coordinate with suitable laboratories, including NABL-accredited facilities where accreditation is applicable to the required scope.

03

Sample handling

Maintain traceability of model numbers, configurations, accessories and samples to avoid report mismatches.

04

Pre-compliance

Identify likely failures early through appropriate engineering and pre-compliance checks before formal testing.

LAB REPORT READINESS

A test report is only useful when it matches the product and the regulatory route.

Before formal submission, verify model identification, ratings, test configuration, applicable standard edition, laboratory scope and report completeness. Requirements vary by scheme and product category.
