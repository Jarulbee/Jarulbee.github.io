# BIS CRS vs ISI

BIS requirements depend on the product category and applicable regulatory framework. The first step is to identify the correct route before arranging testing or preparing an application.

## Start with product applicability

Identify the exact product, model family, technical characteristics and intended market. Then map those characteristics against applicable Indian requirements.

## Testing and documentation

Testing should be planned around the applicable standard and certification route. Documentation should be checked before submission to avoid preventable delays.
