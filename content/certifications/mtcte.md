# Mandatory Testing & Certification of Telecom Equipment

Jarulbee India provides structured guidance for the **MTCTE** route, including applicability review, documentation readiness, testing coordination and submission support.

## Applicability

The exact requirements depend on the product, model, technical characteristics and applicable regulatory framework.

## Process

1. Product and regulatory applicability review.
2. Documentation and testing-gap assessment.
3. Laboratory testing where applicable.
4. Application preparation and submission.
5. Query and clarification support.
6. Approval or registration and post-approval guidance.

## Important Note

Regulatory requirements and timelines can change. Confirm current applicable requirements before making commercial commitments.
